THE RESEARCH FRAMEWORK

From cleaner air
to better decisions.

An air-quality intervention has a cost. Its value depends on the outcomes it changes, the strength of the evidence and whose costs and benefits are included.

CASCADE connects these questions in an explicit economic framework. It combines a targeted narrative synthesis with hypothetical demonstrations to examine the conditions under which a school clean-air investment could justify its costs.

The manuscript is a methodological framework, not a calibrated evaluation of a representative school. Its 16 purposively appraised reports do not constitute a completed systematic review. The AI-assisted appraisals await independent human verification.

THE CENTRAL DISTINCTION

Each link needs its own evidence.

01DELIVERY

What changes in the room?

Outdoor ventilation and filtration change different aspects of indoor air. Effective delivery depends on operating conditions. Filtration does not remove CO₂.

Specify the achieved exposure change, not just a device rating or ventilation target.

02OUTCOMES

What changes for pupils?

Particle concentrations, infections, asthma symptoms, absence, short cognitive tasks and academic achievement measure different endpoints.

An effect on one endpoint does not establish an effect on another.

03VALUATION

What is that change worth?

Healthcare resources, caregiver time, health valuation and any durable achievement effect require separate assumptions and compatible evidence.

Count each consequence once, for a stated population and time horizon.

Inputs and correspondence with the manuscript

The demonstrations assume 25 pupils and one adult, a five-year horizon and service life, 3% discounting, capital of 1,000 MU and annual operations of 200 MU. S1 and S2 use baseline effective clean air of 4 L/s/person, added flow of 505.44 m³/hour, four infection episodes per pupil per year and an addressable fraction of 7.5%, under the low-dose steady-state approximation.

Child-health valuation is 20 MU of expected healthcare resources plus 0.002 QALYs valued at 30,000 MU/QALY per episode. S2 adds 150 MU of caregiver time per episode. S5 directly applies an invented null episode-rate ratio of 1. No study is claimed to supply these inputs.

The manuscript pays capital at the start and operations at each year-end. The calculator annualises capital over the asset life. Their present costs agree for these examples because the five-year horizon equals the five-year asset life and both use the same discount rate. This equivalence does not extend automatically to other horizons or replacement schedules.

INTERPRETING THE FRAMEWORK

Ask what would need to be true.

A threshold is a requirement

The infection reduction or terminal achievement difference needed to break even can be calculated without asserting that an intervention achieves it. A small learning threshold is not evidence of a durable gain.

More removal need not mean more net value

The manuscript’s constructed package comparisons account for overlapping exposure reduction. Added costs can outweigh the value of additional removal. Summing the benefits of separately evaluated interventions would miss that overlap.

Operating times matter

A separate manuscript demonstration models exposure over time. Equal total delivered airflow can produce different doses when operation occurs at different times relative to the source. The browser calculator assumes constant, steady-state conditions.

A positive result remains conditional

Results depend on effect transferability, baseline burden, costs and valuation. They are not automatically school-budget savings. Unvalued outcomes can be beneficial or adverse, so omitted effects do not establish a lower bound.

A SEPARATE POLICY APPLICATION

An older New Zealand classroom.

The NZ case describes a defined classroom with assumed limited winter ventilation. It estimates filtration costs and break-even absence requirements in NZD using dated supplier figures and explicit allowances. It does not supply empirical health or learning benefits to the manuscript.
