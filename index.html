What counts as a matched study?

Match population, intervention, comparator, endpoint and follow-up. For recurrent infections, use an episode-rate ratio; for absence, a ratio of expected days. A risk of any infection or an odds ratio cannot automatically be applied to episode counts. An absence effect cannot be turned into infections. The airflow and addressable-fraction controls do not modify a matched total effect.

03 / HOW IT WORKS

Every result has
a traceable path.

One stable cohort. One matched endpoint. A stated horizon. Costs and valued outcomes discounted to the same date.

Read the research framework ↗
01

Estimate the physical change

Airflow route: episodes × addressable fraction × hypothetical risk reduction. Study route: the matched baseline endpoint × (1 − RR). These routes are alternatives.

02

Value only the outcomes included

Separate healthcare, caregiver time and optional health valuation. Report days and QALYs without implying that they are observed. Count an optional terminal achievement difference once.

03

Compare at a common date

Annual service cost = capital × capital-recovery factor + annual operations. Discount year-end flows across the horizon. Net present value = valued outcomes − costs.

Scope, equations and exclusions

Q₀ = 3.6 × occupants × baseline L/s/person. Low-dose reduction = 1 − Q₀/(Q₀ + added flow). The optional exponential calculation uses P = 1 − exp(−dose) with dose scaled by that flow ratio. Both assume constant, well-mixed, steady-state exposure, not intermittent operation or near-field transmission. Enter effective delivered flow under those conditions, not a duty-cycle-adjusted rating.

Capital recovery = r/[1 − (1+r)−L], with 1/L at r=0. Annual service costing is a service-equivalent comparison; it does not represent the upfront purchase budget or automatically assume zero salvage after a shorter horizon. Annual outcomes and burdens are held constant for the same cohort.

This tool excludes onward transmission, automatic attendance-to-earnings benefits, fiscal funding transfers, and unvalued noise, thermal, chronic-health or other effects. Their omission does not make the result a lower bound. Matched-study effects need justification before repeating them over this horizon. A positive net value under chosen inputs is not a finding that an intervention is cost-beneficial in practice.
