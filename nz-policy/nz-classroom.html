Cost allowances and delivery

Delivery is a sensitivity assumption, not a measured derating factor. It changes particle removal only. Power is held at the selected speed specification. Remaining fixed inputs appear in the cost record below.

The classroom in use

A single-cell Years 4–6 primary room with timber framing and windows on opposite walls. The case assumes constrained winter opening and heating that supplies no outdoor air.

Measure representative occupied days across weather conditions. Record CO₂, temperature, window use and occupancy; verify airflow separately. A single CO₂ reading cannot identify an exact L/s/person value.

MoE guidance suggests more ventilation when CO₂ exceeds 800 ppm for over an hour, and property adviser contact for sustained levels above 2,000 ppm. Existing school IEM devices may support this work. [S2]

Before using this for a decision

  1. Confirm the room, winter airflow and feasible ventilation improvements.
  2. Obtain quotes covering GST, freight, upkeep and commissioning. Replace the assumed electricity tariff with the school contract.
  3. Verify classroom sound, delivered particle removal and hours actually switched on.
  4. Choose an endpoint-specific causal effect and non-overlapping NZ resource values. Keep null and adverse-effect scenarios.

The benchmark device is not a procurement recommendation. This case does not rank filtration against ventilation remediation or extrapolate to all NZ schools.

Transparent calculation

Present cost = upfront cost + yearly operating allowance × the five-year annuity factor.

Annual equivalent = present cost ÷ annuity factor. Break-even pupil days = annual equivalent ÷ assumed value per day.

Use 2% real discounting and the Treasury's 8% non-commercial sensitivity [S6]. No inflation escalation or salvage value. Classroom pupil places remain constant; this is not five-year follow-up of the same primary pupils. Avoid counting caregiver time both per illness and per absence day.
